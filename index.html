Privacy Policy

Boutique Getaway Publisher is an internal tool. Boutique Getaway Group LLC uses it to publish its own marketing videos and photos for its vacation rental in Tobyhanna, Pennsylvania to its own TikTok and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the tool accesses

When the account owner connects TikTok or Instagram, the tool receives an access token and basic profile information (account ID and username) for that account only. It uses these solely to upload and publish content that the account owner has approved.

What the tool does not do

  • It does not collect data about other users, followers, viewers or guests.
  • It does not read messages, comments or contacts.
  • It does not sell, share or rent any data to third parties.
  • It does not use data for advertising or profiling.

Storage and retention

Access tokens are stored privately in the owner's Google Apps Script project settings and are never published. Content files are stored in the owner's private Google Drive and private GitHub repository. The owner can revoke access at any time from the TikTok or Instagram app settings, after which the stored token stops working.
